Reading Practice — system design

A read-aloud game inside ReadAloud for grade 6 through advanced readers. The speech server’s Whisper transcript is lined up against the passage to judge every word; Gemini Decision 1 listens to the recording for phrasing, expression and pace; Jev screens passages; everything runs through the existing web server in the kind cluster.

C4 · container view

What talks to what

The browser never holds a key. The web server pod forwards to Google (Gemini Decision 1) and TypeSafe (Jev) with keys from the library volume or the readaloud-secrets Secret, so the game adds one route, /api/decide, and no new Terraform resources. VoiceStudio stays on the Mac mini, outside k8s.

BPMN · process

One round of play

Passages loop through Jev until one fits the band and is safe. The recording is the only handoff to the judge: the speech server transcribes it for the words while Gemini rates how it was read. Words a transcript can’t be trusted on (a misheard name, one short word for another) become “unclear” in Score & save and are offered for a retry rather than counted wrong.

Sequence · messages

Screening, then judging

Two System One calls per round: a small text request to Jev per candidate passage, then one audio request to Gemini Decision 1 with three questions: phrasing, expression and pace. The words are judged from a Whisper transcript made by VoiceStudio through /api/vs/transcribe at the same time; that call and tap-to-hear (VoiceStudio through /api/vs) are left out to keep the diagram readable.
